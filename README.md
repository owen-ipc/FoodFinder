FoodFinder is an app designed to help make the most of your money and prepare delicious homemade meals.

Any craving you have, you can put into the app to look for the cheapest options for making it. There's no chemicals or poorly-made processed food when you make the food yourself. There are over 1000 dishes in our catalogue, with images added to them to get an idea of what your final output will look like. Each recipe is comprised of ingredients from a dataset from a Target near campus, so you won't be looking for ingredients that aren't there. The three of us are excited to help you make the healthiest, best food you've tasted while also saving you money. We believe that everyone should be able to eat without having to worry about the price of the food.
